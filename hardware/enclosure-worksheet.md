# Enclosure measurement worksheet

This package does not include a fit-validated STL. The actual battery, antenna, microphone port and PCB clearances must be measured before an enclosure can be responsibly finalized. You can use this sheet with your preferred CAD tool.

The board is specified as **21 × 17.8 mm** [S1]. The selected battery is listed as **32.5 × 25 × 5 mm** [S5]. These are component specifications, not guaranteed finished enclosure dimensions. Allow space for the cable, PCB components, antenna and soldered joints. Do not rely on the earlier speculative 36 × 28 mm enclosure estimate.

Use a guarded, slightly recessed button. Provide a clear acoustic path to the microphone; do not cover its port with adhesive. Position the antenna away from the battery and large conductors, following its actual antenna guidance. Keep metal fasteners and sharp print features away from the pouch cell. Do not clamp, puncture, bend or squeeze the battery. Provide connector access so the cell can be disconnected for service. Enclosure closure must not apply force to the board's antenna connector.

Begin with an open tray or oversized enclosure. Record voice and range tests before reducing dimensions. Verify charging temperature with the lid installed; a closed printed case changes heat dissipation. Print material, layer orientation and clip strength remain your mechanical design decisions.
