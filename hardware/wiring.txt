HERMES VOICE BUTTON — WIRING NETLIST v0.1
Board: Seeed Studio XIAO nRF54LM20A SENSE (not plain non-Sense)

BATT_POS: protected 1S 3.7 V LiPo positive -> mating lead -> BAT+
BATT_NEG: protected LiPo negative -> mating lead -> BAT- / GND
CAPTURE:  D0 / P1.00 -> momentary switch contact group A
GROUND:   GND -> momentary switch contact group B
ANTENNA:  supplied 2.4 GHz FPC IPEX4 -> board antenna connector
USB:      USB-C for USB programming and charge input

Switch wiring uses a normally-open contact pair and internal pull-up.
Do not connect the switch to BAT+, 5V, RESET, or microphone pins.
Check contact pairs with a meter; same-side tactile-switch legs may be common.
Solder only the disconnected board-side pigtail; verify battery polarity before mating.
No speaker, external microphone, phone microphone, or extra charger board.
Charge profile intended: 100 mA / 4.20 V; generated DTS and physical measurement required.
No real cell temperature measurement: board NTC is a fixed resistor in the referenced schematic.
